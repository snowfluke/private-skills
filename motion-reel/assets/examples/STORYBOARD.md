# Beaverflow exhibition reel

Loop: 72 beats at 140 BPM = 18 bars = 30.86 s. 1920 × 1080, 30 fps. No voice, no subtitles.
The last frame and the first frame are the same picture: one teal line across the middle.

Every section shows a real part of the platform. Every cut is a hand-off: one object carries
across the boundary and becomes the first thing of the next section.

Exhibition rules: every bar reads on its own from a distance (words ≥ 120 px, one idea per bar,
high contrast). Rows and log lines are texture, not reading matter. No timecode HUD.
