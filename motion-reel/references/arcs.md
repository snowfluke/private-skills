# Arcs and finales

Pick the arc that fits the product's shape. Map the product's own steps onto it.
Share of runtime is a guide, not a rule. Keep any act the product cannot fill
out of the film.

| Arc | Fits when | Shape | Finales that fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| `journey` | One input becomes one output through several steps | Follow one object (a file, a request, a photo) from raw to done. Each step is a place it passes through. | `trace-the-path`, `zoom-out-reveal` |
| `before-after` | The product removes a clear pain | The old way, slow and messy, then the same task with the product, fast. Split screen or a hard cut on the beat. | `split-merge`, `type-lockup` |
| `scale-reveal` | The value is volume or reach | Start with one item, then pull back: ten, a thousand, a million, on the beat. | `zoom-out-reveal`, `mosaic-resolve` |
| `build-from-nothing` | The product is a builder or an editor | An empty canvas fills as the user builds; each part is explained as it lands. | `collapse-to-mark`, `type-lockup` |
| `race` | The value is speed against an alternative | Two lanes run the same job; the product's lane finishes while the other is still going. | `split-merge`, `type-lockup` |
| `day-in-the-life` | The product runs in the background | Morning to night: the schedule fires, results arrive, a person acts on one. | `loop-to-start`, `trace-the-path` |
| `question-answer` | The product answers questions or finds things | A question appears; the product searches, narrows, and lands on the answer. Repeat with harder questions. | `type-lockup`, `zoom-out-reveal` |
| `catalog` | The product is a toolkit with many equal features | Feature after feature at an accelerating cut rate, each with one real screen. | `montage-accelerate`, `mosaic-resolve` |

## Finales

| Finale | Shape |
|---|---|
| `collapse-to-mark` | Everything on screen shrinks into one shape, and the mark grows from it. |
| `montage-accelerate` | Feature cards cut faster and faster (1, 1/2, 1/4, 1/8 beat), then stop dead on the mark. |
| `zoom-out-reveal` | The camera pulls back from one detail until it shows the whole system, then the mark. |
| `split-merge` | Two halves of the frame slide together and become the mark. |
| `type-lockup` | The tagline builds word by word on the beat, and the mark lands on the last word. |
| `mosaic-resolve` | Many small tiles of the film resolve into the mark's shape. |
| `trace-the-path` | The path the object travelled draws once more in one line, and ends on the mark. |
| `loop-to-start` | The last frame becomes the first one, so the film can loop. |

The opening, the middle and the finale all use the film's hero motion from
`DIRECTION.json` for their transitions.
