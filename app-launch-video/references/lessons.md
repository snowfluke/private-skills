# Lessons from the first production

This skill came from one session. It produced a launch film for a financial
reconciliation platform, from 2026-09-25 21:30 to 2026-09-26 16:00 (UTC+7), in
three Claude sessions: 42 prompts in the main session and 10 in a short-form
side session. Each row below is one prompt or one burst of prompts (shortened),
the general rule it teaches, and the reason. The product names
appear only as evidence. Apply the rule, not the example.

## How the work moved

```
v1 (HyperFrames, 90 s demo) --"too slow, a demo"--> v2 (brag-slim, commercial)
                                                   \
                    v3 (Remotion mascot reel, side)  \
v1 story + v2 look ---------------------------------> v4 (kept, 152 s)
                                                     then 11 revision rounds
```

Expect the same shape. The first cut is too slow and reads as a demo. Keep every
variant, because the client will merge the best parts of two of them.

Idle holds before and after the morph added 2.4 s. |

## Standing preferences seen across rounds

- The client sends numbered revision lists. Answer each number, in order.
- The client does not want to pick layouts. Decide the UX yourself. Ask only
  about facts: slogans, names, which step matters most.
- Squeezing is welcome. Adding content and cutting idle time both beat a slow
  pace.
- Screenshots of the live app are the ground truth for layout.
